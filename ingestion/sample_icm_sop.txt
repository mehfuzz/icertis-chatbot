Icertis Contract Management (ICM) - Standard Operating Procedures

1. CONTRACT CREATION

1.1 Creating a New Contract
- Navigate to Contracts > Create New Contract
- Select the contract type (Master Service Agreement, Purchase Order, NDA, etc.)
- Fill in the mandatory fields: Contract Name, Counterparty, Effective Date, Expiry Date
- Upload supporting documents if required
- Assign the contract to the appropriate business unit and module
- Click "Submit for Review" to move the contract to the approval workflow

1.2 Contract Templates
- ICM provides pre-approved templates for standard contract types
- Navigate to Templates > Browse Templates to find approved templates
- Templates ensure compliance with Airtel's legal standards
- Customizations to templates require Legal team approval

2. CONTRACT APPROVAL WORKFLOW

2.1 Approval Stages
- Stage 1: Business Owner Review (1-2 business days)
- Stage 2: Legal Review (3-5 business days for new templates, 1 day for standard)
- Stage 3: Finance Approval (for contracts above INR 10 lakhs)
- Stage 4: Management Sign-off (for contracts above INR 1 crore)

2.2 Escalation Process
- If a reviewer does not act within the SLA, the contract auto-escalates to their manager
- Escalations are visible in the Dashboard under "Pending Approvals"
- To manually escalate, click "Escalate" on the contract detail page

3. CONTRACT AMENDMENTS

3.1 Raising an Amendment
- Open the original contract
- Click "Create Amendment"
- Specify the sections being amended and provide justification
- Amendments follow the same approval workflow as new contracts

3.2 Version Control
- ICM maintains a full version history of all contracts
- Previous versions are accessible via the "Version History" tab
- Only the latest approved version is the active contract

4. CONTRACT RENEWAL

4.1 Automatic Renewal Alerts
- ICM sends email alerts 90, 60, and 30 days before contract expiry
- Alerts are sent to the Contract Owner and their manager
- Navigate to Contracts > Expiring Soon to see all contracts needing attention

4.2 Renewal Process
- Open the expiring contract
- Click "Initiate Renewal"
- Update the new effective and expiry dates
- Renewal goes through a streamlined approval workflow (Legal review optional for unchanged terms)

5. TROUBLESHOOTING COMMON ISSUES

5.1 Contract Stuck in Approval
- Check if the assigned approver is on leave (Out-of-Office delegation needed)
- Verify the approver has ICM login access
- Contact ICM support at icm-support@airtel.com for system issues

5.2 Cannot Find a Contract
- Use Advanced Search with Contract ID, Counterparty name, or date range
- Check if the contract was archived (Contracts > Archived)
- Verify you have the correct access permissions for that contract

5.3 Signature Issues with DocuSign Integration
- Ensure the signatories' email addresses are correct in the contract
- DocuSign envelopes expire after 30 days; re-initiate if expired
- For bulk signing issues, contact the ICM admin team

6. ACCESS AND PERMISSIONS

6.1 Requesting Access
- Submit an access request via ServiceNow: Category = ICM Access
- Specify the required role: Viewer, Creator, Approver, or Admin
- Access is provisioned within 2 business days

6.2 Role Descriptions
- Viewer: Read-only access to contracts in assigned business unit
- Creator: Can create and edit contracts, cannot approve
- Approver: Can approve contracts within delegated authority limits
- Admin: Full access including configuration and user management
